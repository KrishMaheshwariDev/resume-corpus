- Develop new applications, fix bugs, and expand existing features.

- Commercial Core Java experience and object-oriented programming.

- 3-4 years of experience with at least 2 years in software development.
- WebSockets, Tomcat, SQL Server, Cassandra, PostgreSQL, JUnit, networking, SCADA, and renewable assets such as wind, solar, or BESS.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related engineering discipline.
